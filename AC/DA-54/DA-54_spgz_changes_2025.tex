Determine the number of changes made to the SPGZ in 2025 for each of
possible scenarios (cases) for the subsequent determination of frequency
of scenarios (cases) in which customers receive notifications (in
accordance with
``\href{https://pm.mos.ru/issue/EAIST_JB-8765}{EAIST\_JB-8765}
Реализовать уведомления пользователям при изменении позиций КПГЗ/СПГЗ,
которые были запланированы в лотах'') and scenarios (cases) that entail
the need for customers to make changes to the lot specifications.
